xvrStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who uses this app

xvrStudio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @xvrGaming. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and video descriptions to the owner's own channel and schedule their publication;
  • read the status of those videos and add them to the channel's playlists;
  • read the channel's own analytics reports (views, watch time, estimated revenue).

It does not access any other channel, any other user's data, or any Google service other than YouTube.

How data is stored

The OAuth token and the downloaded reports are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. No data is sent to any server operated by the app, and no data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

xvrStudio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can also delete the locally stored token and reports.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the @xvrGaming channel page.
